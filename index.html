THE CODEX

How to Play

Classic dueling without the newer summoning systems.

01

Victory & Decks

Both duelists start at 8,000 Life Points with 5 cards in hand. Reduce your opponent's LP to zero, or win when they must draw but cannot. Your main deck has 40–60 cards, up to 3 of each card. Your Extra Deck holds up to 15 Fusion Monsters.

02

Turn Structure

Draw → Standby → Main 1 → Battle → Main 2 → End. The first player skips their first draw and cannot battle on turn one. You can normally summon or set once per turn.

03

Stars & Tribute

Level 1–4 monsters can be played normally. Level 5–6 requires sacrificing 1 of your monsters; Level 7+ requires 2. Summon face-up in Attack Position or Set face-down in Defense Position.

04

Battle

You have 5 monster zones and 5 spell/trap zones. Each Attack Position monster normally attacks once per turn. Versus Attack Position, compare ATK with ATK and deal the difference. Versus Defense Position, compare ATK with DEF; only the attacker takes difference damage when weaker.

05

Spells & Traps

Spells generally activate during a Main Phase. Set Traps can activate from the following turn. Quick-Play Spells can be used at faster timings. Continuous, Equip, Field and Counter cards are part of the planned full rules engine.

06

Traditional Fusion

Play Relic Fusion while you have the exact named materials in your hand or on the field. Send them to the Graveyard and summon the matching Fusion Monster from your Extra Deck. No Link, Xyz, Synchro, Pendulum or Ritual cards.

Alpha mechanics notice. The solo prototype implements deck limits, levels and tributes, battle calculation, basic spells/traps, phases and traditional fusion. Full player-controlled Chains, Spell Speeds, priority windows, all continuous/equip/field interactions, tournament rulings and online multiplayer are not yet implemented. This is an independent original game, not an official Yu-Gi-Oh! product.
